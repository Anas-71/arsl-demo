About this demo

This page recognizes the 32 static hand shapes of the Arabic Sign Language alphabet from your webcam, one letter at a time, and builds words from them. When it is not sure, it says "not recognized" rather than guessing.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. The camera video is never uploaded or stored: there is no server behind this page, only the files it is made of. Nothing you sign or type is saved.

How to use it

  • Click Open camera and allow camera access.
  • Show one hand, fully in the frame, with good light and a plain background.
  • Hold a sign for about a second until it is added to the word.
  • To repeat a letter, lower your hand briefly, then sign it again.
  • Not sure how a letter is signed? Use Spell a word or All letters to see the hand shapes.

How it works

  1. MediaPipe Hand Landmarker finds 21 points on your hand in each video frame.
  2. The points are turned into 42 numbers that describe the hand's shape, independent of where the hand is, how big it looks, and whether it is a left or right hand.
  3. A small trained classifier gives a probability for each of the 32 letters.
  4. A letter is shown only when it is likely enough and stays the same for most of the last 8 frames, so flickering guesses never reach the word.
